Which class to start with

Start with one ranger or one mage.

One fighter

Lone Wolf gives 3× gold, xp and luck while only one fighter of your account is online.

Range is safety

70 px with a bow, 170 px with a staff. A simple bot that stands still and attacks survives.

Merchant later

A merchant gets no xp from kills. Add one when you have loot to sell.

Source: G.classes[cls].description; node/logic/encouragement.js:223-271

G calls the mage "ideal for beginners" and the ranger "very suitable for beginners". Pick a warrior if you want the most HP and damage at level 1, but it must stand next to the monster. G says the priest and the rogue are not ideal for beginners; a priest does little damage alone.
