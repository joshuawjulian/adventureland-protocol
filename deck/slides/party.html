Parties

How a party shares a kill

Every member gets xp

Each member on the server gets its share of each kill, at any distance.

The share

Damage, heals ×1.8 and a quarter of damage taken, plus a fixed 36,000. Priests ×1.36. Merchants 0.

Loot

Each chest item goes to one member by share. Gold is divided by share.

To make a party, send party with event invite and a name; the other side sends accept. The server adds the scores for each account and divides by the number of that account's characters in the party, so your own characters share one account's share. The contribution score decays by 20% each minute. A merchant in the party does not reduce the fighters' xp and never gets chest items.
