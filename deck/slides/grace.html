Grace: the hidden luck counters

Bad luck raises your next chance

Item grace

Stays on the item. Grows with higher scrolls, offerings, at random, and while the item is worn.

Player grace

One for each level. Each failure raises it. A success at that level sets it to 0.

Server grace

Shared by all players on the server. Starts at 24. Any player's success resets it.

Offering grace

Rises when an attempt with an offering fails. Falls after a success.

Ask first

Most inputs are hidden. Send upgrade with calculate: true. The server returns the exact chance and uses nothing.

Also hidden: one secret "lucky" inventory slot makes your roll better 60% of the time.

Source: node/server.js:7325-7400, 7457-7487; node/server_functions.js:44-52, 3193-3199

Grace is a pity system: it stops very long series of failures. The total that the formula uses is capped per level. The lucky slot is player.p.item_num, selected at random one time; the server log calls it "16 cheat". If the roll's first four decimals match the chance, a success gives the lucky property and achievement.
