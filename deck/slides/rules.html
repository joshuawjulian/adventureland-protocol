Many characters and bots

Bots are fine. The limits are on characters.

3 + 1

Three fighters and one merchant of an account online at a time.

One account per player

All accounts of one player share the same limits.

80% for itself

An account must serve itself 80% of the time or more.

An active person

Accounts that run 24/7 with no contact with players can get a ban for a time.

No real-money trade

Do not sell gold or items for money.

Report bugs

Do not use them. A report gets a Bug Bounty Box.

From the official guide. Read the live rules before your bot depends on them.

Source: docs/guide/limits.html, htmls/contents/terms.html; is_player_allowed node/server_functions.js:383-437

The exact online limit is in a private configuration file, so the live number is unclear from the source. The server also limits players per IP address; Steam and Mac App Store accounts get a much higher limit. A Licence to Kill removes the limits for 7 minutes. Two or more online fighters end the Lone Wolf bonus.
