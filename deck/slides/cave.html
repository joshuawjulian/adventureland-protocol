Bring all members to Dorr, within 160 px. Kill or leave all monsters that target a member; all members must be alive. Then send enter with place dreams. The cave gives axes, scythes and Cave Amber, which Leo crafts into rare gear. A custom client needs extra work here: each member connects with map_protocol=1, and the server sends cave events for choices and story text.
