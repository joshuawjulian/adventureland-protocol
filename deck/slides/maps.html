Maps and the world

The main maps

Mainland: the hub, with almost all shops.

Winterland: the second town. Bees, boars, wolves, the Ice Golem.

Desertland: the third town. Scorpions, Fire Spirits, the Ent.

The Underground: tunnels of strong, aggressive monsters.

Key dungeons: Crypt, Tomb, Spider Den, Lair of the Dark Mage.

Night: 00:00 to 05:59 server time. Monsters move at 70% speed.

Source: G.maps (G 17478); node/server.js:1828-1830

Safe maps (the bank, the Wizard's Crib) allow no hostile attacks. PvP maps such as the Arena allow other players to kill you. Most bosses in key dungeons do not respawn, so a dead boss stays dead. Do not walk through the Underground without preparation.
