Green bars are 95% and above; gold bars are the levels where failures become normal. The scroll grade must be equal to or more than the current grade of the item: a Blade at +7 is High, so +8 needs a scroll1. The result comes some seconds later as a hitchhiker game_response, upgrade_success or upgrade_fail. Upgrades are the largest gold sink in the game.
