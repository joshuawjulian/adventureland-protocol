Kill counters call out rare monsters

After a random number of kills on the server, the rare monster appears among the usual ones. S does not show them.

Each counter is random in the range shown. Only the announce flag of the monster tells players about them. Only one Many Eye, Mimic or Pale Dino can be alive at a time. Other things to know: monster hunts from Daisy give monster tokens; the Tracktrix records kills and can call the cavalry, four NPC fighters, for help.
