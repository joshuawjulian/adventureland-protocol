Drops and loot

The loot goes to whom the monster attacks

That is usually the tank. Open chests at once: after 8 minutes, or from more than 400 px, the gold bonus is lost.

Source: node/server.js:2325-2334, 2869-2893, 11273-11470

For each entry in G.drops.monsters[type], the item drops when the test on the slide is true. share is 1 for a usual kill; luckm is 1 plus luck divided by 100. In a party, each item goes to one member at random by share, and gold is divided by share with the opener's multiplier, so let the member with the most gold gear open chests. Cooperative bosses give each contributor its own chest.
