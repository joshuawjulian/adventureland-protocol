The world

Maps seen from above, measured in pixels

A map has walls, doors to other maps, NPCs and monster spawn areas. G.geometry holds the walls as lines.

Mainland (main) is the hub. Its town is near (0, 0). A new character starts at (−87, 673), in the south part of the town.

A spawn area is the box where one monster type lives. A dead monster returns after a few seconds.

All characters on a server share the same monsters, so other players can make a good area crowded.

54 maps are in G. The server uses 49 of them.

Source: G.maps, G.geometry (G 17478); api.js:496 · Map art from the game's own guide

The unit of distance is the pixel. A spawn area is the boundary of an entry in G.maps[map].monsters, as x1, y1, x2, y2. A monster that nobody attacks slowly becomes stronger, which comes later in the leveling section.
