Rings, earrings, amulets, belts and orbs compound instead of upgrading. They come only from drops, crafts and exchanges. Use three items with the same name and level, plus a compound scroll of the right grade, at Cue. The result arrives after about 10 seconds; on a success the item is in the first slot. Keep +0 copies until you have a group of three.
