What Adventure Land is

A small 2D MMORPG, played by code

Most MMOs ban bots. Adventure Land welcomes them.

The official client has a code editor, CODE. Your JavaScript calls functions such as attack(target) and move(x, y), and the character plays without you.

Players compete on the quality of their code, not on the speed of their hands.

A custom client goes one step further: it replaces the official client and sends the socket events itself.

Adventure Land game guide · What Adventure Land is

An MMORPG is a game world that many people share at the same time. Each person controls one or more characters. In Adventure Land, a bot is the normal way to play. The Learn course on the site does not write scripts for the official client: it replaces the client and speaks the protocol directly. To write a good bot, you must know the game that the protocol describes. That is what this deck is about.
