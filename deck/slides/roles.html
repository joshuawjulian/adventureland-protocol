Party roles

Four jobs in a party

Tank

Warrior, paladin

Takes the hits. Taunt moves a monster from an ally to the tank.

Healer

Priest

Keeps the others alive. "Every serious party needs at least one priest."

DPS

Ranger, mage, rogue

Kills quickly. Rangers and mages hit from a distance.

Economy

Merchant

Does not fight. Sells loot, upgrades gear and keeps the gold.

CompositionWhy
Ranger or mage, plus a merchantThe start. Easy to code, and Lone Wolf gives 3×.
Warrior, priest, mage or ranger, merchantThe usual party. It survives almost all monsters that its gear allows.
Ranger, priest, mage, merchantNo tank. This works when monsters die before they arrive.
