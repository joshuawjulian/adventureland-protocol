Before you go to the next monster: its damage per second must be less than the HP that you get back. The free regen_hp gives 12.5 HP per second, an hpot0 gives 100 per second but costs 20 gold. Monsters with aggro 1 attack first, so more than one can attack you at the same time. Spend the gold: keep about 2,000 for potions, buy gloves, a coat and pants from Gabriel, and upgrade each piece to +3 at Cue.
