From level 1 to the endgame

Five stages

1-20

Start

Goos, bees and crabs near the town. Basic gear. Upgrades to +3 almost always work.

20-50

Growth

Caves, Winterland, Desertland. Merchant's Luck at 40. Gear starts to matter more than level.

50-70

Midgame

The Underground. Cleave, 3-Shot, Absorb Sins. Upgrades past +6 fail more often than not.

70-90

Late game

Hundreds of millions of xp per level. Progress comes from gear and key dungeons.

90+

Endgame

World and instance bosses, rare items, gear at +10 and higher, and trade.

After the first few dozen levels, gear sets the limit more than level does. Progress is a loop: farm gold and items, then change them into better gear.

The level ranges are estimates for this guide, from the xp curve and monster stats. They are not official.

Adventure Land has no gear score in G or the server. A bot that needs one must make its own, for example the damage per second it does and takes against one monster.
