A selection of the skills that change how a bot plays. 3-Shot and 5-Shot cost only the MP of the skill, so rangers are very good against groups of weak monsters. Merchant's Luck gives any player +12 luck for one hour. War Cry and Dark Blessing are party buffs to use whenever they are ready. The full table per class is in the guide.
