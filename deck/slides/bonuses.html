Bonuses for new and solo players

Multipliers that you can plan for

BonusWhenGold / xp / luck
New PlayerThe first 40 days after your first character×5 on days 1-10, down to ×1.5
Lone WolfOnly one fighter of your account is online×3
Welcome BackYou return after more than 60 days away×3 (×2 from level 80)

They multiply

When two apply, the server uses their product. A party gives its own bonus: +10% xp with 2 fighters, up to +40% with 9.

Caution: authfail

The authfail condition stops all three bonuses. Link a platform id first.

Source: node/logic/encouragement.js:214-407; node/server.js:1186-1188

New Player by period: days 1 to 10 give 5, 5, 5; days 11 to 20 give 2, 4, 4; days 21 to 30 give 2, 2, 3; days 31 to 40 give 1.5 each. The xp part stops at level 80. The server pays the extra in proportion to your contribution to each monster, so for a monster you kill alone the result is close to the full multiplier. An account group with 25 or more characters gets no bonus.
