A merchant in practice

Open a stand in the town square

  1. Create a merchant. Send it 40,000 gold from a fighter: no fee between your own characters.
  2. Buy a stand (stand0) from Divian at (−193, 680).
  3. Go to the square near (0, 0). Open the stand with merchant.
  4. List items above their NPC value, or the NPC is the better buyer.
  5. Keep one listing open for 2 minutes: Merrit brings a Market Parcel, once each hour.

For each item of loot, in order

Keep what you need. Compound jewelry. Send better gear to a fighter. Stand for valuable items. Sell the rest to an NPC at once.

Source: node/server.js:1756, 3468; node/logic/market_patron.js; node/server_functions.js:4282-4299

A stand has 16 trade slots, 24 for a merchant at level 70 or with a computer stand, and 30 at level 80. A character on a stand moves at speed 10, and an attack closes the stand. Close it with merchant close: true before you walk. Xyn exchanges the parcel; the most probable results are 5 upgrade scrolls, 2 compound scrolls or a high upgrade scroll. Merchants get xp from trades, donations and an open stand, not from kills.
