What Adventure Land is

A small 2D MMORPG, played by code

Most MMOs ban bots. Adventure Land welcomes them.

The official client has a code editor, CODE. Your JavaScript calls functions such as attack(target) and move(x, y), and the character plays without you.

Players compete on the quality of their code, not on the speed of their hands.

A custom client goes one step further: it replaces the official client and sends the socket events itself.

The Mainland town, the hub of the world.

Adventure Land game guide · What Adventure Land is

An MMORPG is a game world that many people share at the same time. Each person controls one or more characters. In Adventure Land, a bot is the normal way to play. The Learn course on the site does not write scripts for the official client: it replaces the client and speaks the protocol directly. To write a good bot, you must know the game that the protocol describes. That is what this deck is about.

The world

Maps seen from above, measured in pixels

A map has walls, doors to other maps, NPCs and monster spawn areas. G.geometry holds the walls as lines.

Mainland (main) is the hub. Its town is near (0, 0). A new character starts at (−87, 673), in the south part of the town.

A spawn area is the box where one monster type lives. A dead monster returns after a few seconds.

All characters on a server share the same monsters, so other players can make a good area crowded.

54 maps are in G. The server uses 49 of them.

Source: G.maps, G.geometry (G 17478); api.js:496 · Map art from the game's own guide

The unit of distance is the pixel. A spawn area is the boundary of an entry in G.maps[map].monsters, as x1, y1, x2, y2. A monster that nobody attacks slowly becomes stronger, which comes later in the leveling section.

Servers, regions and PvP

Each server is its own copy of the world

A server key is a region plus a name: EUI, USII, USPVP. Each server has its own monsters, bosses and event state (S).

RegionClock offset
EUUTC +1 h
USUTC −5 h
ASIAUTC +7 h

Keep all your fighters on one server, and make it their home.

PvP servers

Every map allows PvP. Xp ×1.2, gold ×1.25, luck ×1.15. Marked items can drop for your killer.

Home server

The first server you join. There you get 5× points on cooperative bosses and extra drop tables. Change it with set_home, once in 36 hours.

Hop Sickness

Level 60 or more on a server that is not home: −80% luck, gold and xp, and −20% damage, for 12 minutes of play.

Source: node/server.js:339-422, 5523-5535, 13311; node/server_functions.js:1094-1104

The region offset sets the hours of night and of the daily events. HARDCORE is a special server: one character per account, large multipliers, and a death costs a full level. Realm Fatigue stops the home bonuses for 30 minutes when another fighter of your account was recently on a different server.

Accounts and characters

Set up once, before your bot plays

  1. Make an account in the desktop client (Steam or the Mac App Store). The website refuses new accounts.
  2. Log in one time through that client. This links a platform id to the account.
  3. Create one character and select its class.
  4. Write down its name. Your program needs it.

Caution: do not skip step 2

Without a platform link, each character gets authfail: −85% luck, −85% gold, −20% xp, and no bonuses for new players.

Slots: 5 for each account, 8 with a platform id, up to 18 at 200 shells each. A name has 4 to 12 characters.

Source: api.js:14-31, 93, 559-563; node/server.js:11863-11869

One account owns many characters. You log in to the account, and then you put one of its characters into the game. A second limit controls how many characters of one account can be online at the same time; that comes at the end of the deck.

MMO parties divide the work into tank, DPS and healer. Adventure Land adds a fourth role, the merchant, which does business and does not fight. Each class changes one attribute into damage: the server adds weapon attack times main stat divided by 20 to the attack of the character. You cannot change the class later.

Which class to start with

Start with one ranger or one mage.

One fighter

Lone Wolf gives 3× gold, xp and luck while only one fighter of your account is online.

Range is safety

70 px with a bow, 170 px with a staff. A simple bot that stands still and attacks survives.

Merchant later

A merchant gets no xp from kills. Add one when you have loot to sell.

Source: G.classes[cls].description; node/logic/encouragement.js:223-271

G calls the mage "ideal for beginners" and the ranger "very suitable for beginners". Pick a warrior if you want the most HP and damage at level 1, but it must stand next to the monster. G says the priest and the rogue are not ideal for beginners; a priest does little damage alone.

Stats and combat

How the server resolves a hit

1

Reflection

Magical hits only. With the target's reflection %, the hit goes back.

2

Evasion

Physical hits only. Evasion % stops it. Players max out at 50.

3

Miss

The attacker's miss % or the target's avoidance %.

4

Movement

Target more than 72 px from the aim point: the hit misses.

5

Damage

Crit, rogue stacks, mob combo, a ±10% roll, then the defense curve.

Step 4 is why kiting works. Move away while you attack, and slow projectiles and melee monsters miss you.

Pure damage skips steps 1 and 2. It has no roll and ignores armor and resistance.

Source: commence_attack node/server.js:3159; complete_attack node/server.js:3707-4057

An attack is a projectile. commence_attack checks that the hit is legal, then sends an action event. When the projectile arrives, complete_attack runs these steps in order. Your client sees steps 1 to 4 as a hit event with damage 0 and one of reflect, evade, miss or avoid. A possible server bug: piercing counts two times, because both the attacker and the projectile carry it.

Aggro, tanks and fear

Why parties need a tank

Passive monsters (aggro 0) never start a fight. Goos and snakes are passive.

Aggressive monsters check every 1.2 s. With the chance aggro, they attack a near player.

A hit makes a monster with no target select its attacker.

Fear starts when more monsters target you than your courage allows. Warriors have 5, most classes 2.

A tank bot's rule: if a monster targets a different party member, taunt it.

FearSpeedAttack
1−20× 0.6
2−40× 0.4
3−70× 0.2

Source: node/server.js:1727-1751, 4390-4398, 13943-13947, 14308-14319

Each monster has one target at a time. Tanking is control of those targets: Taunt and Agitate (warrior) and Absorb Sins (priest) pull monsters to one character. A target with more than 3 monsters also gets a combo multiplier on each hit, so a crowd of weak monsters does much more damage than their attack values show. Keep your characters a few pixels apart: characters in the same 6 by 6 pixel cell can all take one hit.

Potions and regeneration

No passive regeneration

HP and MP return only from potions, the free regen abilities, heals, lifesteal and a new level. All of them share one potion cooldown.

Use the free abilities with the use event, item hp or mp. Drink a potion with equip and the inventory slot of the potion. Each normal attack and each heal also costs mp_cost; a mage at level 80 pays 45 MP for each attack. Without MP, an attack fails with no_mp.

Death and respawn

When HP reaches 0

Xp loss: the larger of 1% of the level's xp and 2% of your current xp. You never lose a level, except on HARDCORE.

Gravestone: you stay dead (rip: true) for at least 12 s. A priest can revive you.

Respawn: back to town with full HP and half MP.

Tome of Protection: cuts the loss to 1/50, then it is used up.

Watch out: each death can give the monster a level.

After a death the world turns gray until you respawn.

Source: node/server.js:224, 6271-6309, 13884-13936

Your own death arrives in your player data as rip: true. The death event is for monsters that die near you. Send respawn after 12 seconds; too early fails with cant_respawn. With safe: true, respawn moves you to the Wizard's Crib. In PvP areas the xp loss is divided by 10.

Your first hour

Level 10, and a bot that does not die

  1. Join a normal server, for example EUI. You start near (−87, 673).
  2. Walk south to the goo area: (−282, 702) to (218, 872).
  3. Attack the nearest goo. Attack again each time the cooldown ends.
  4. HP 50 below the maximum: use the free regen_hp. Below half: drink an hpot0.
  5. Level 2 after 2 goos. Level 10 after about 49 goos, or up to 5× faster with the new-player bonus.

A goo has 100 HP and gives 100 xp at level 1. This one gained levels while nobody fought it.

Errors to expect: cooldown, too_far, no_mp, not_ready · Source: G.maps.main, G.levels

This path is for one ranger or mage. After each kill your xp grows by 100 and a chest appears; open it. If you see cooldown, wait for the ms in the reply. too_far means move closer: a mage hits from 170 px, a ranger from 70 px. no_mp: use regen_mp or an MP potion. not_ready: the shared potion cooldown has not ended. rip true: the character died; respawn after 12 seconds.

From level 1 to the endgame

Five stages

1-20

Start

Goos, bees and crabs near the town. Basic gear. Upgrades to +3 almost always work.

20-50

Growth

Caves, Winterland, Desertland. Merchant's Luck at 40. Gear starts to matter more than level.

50-70

Midgame

The Underground. Cleave, 3-Shot, Absorb Sins. Upgrades past +6 fail more often than not.

70-90

Late game

Hundreds of millions of xp per level. Progress comes from gear and key dungeons.

90+

Endgame

World and instance bosses, rare items, gear at +10 and higher, and trade.

After the first few dozen levels, gear sets the limit more than level does. Progress is a loop: farm gold and items, then change them into better gear.

The level ranges are estimates for this guide, from the xp curve and monster stats. They are not official.

Adventure Land has no gear score in G or the server. A bot that needs one must make its own, for example the damage per second it does and takes against one monster.

Maps and the world

Six ways to travel

Walk with move inside a map. The server refuses moves through walls, so your bot needs its own pathfinding.

Doors connect maps. Go within 112 px, then send transport.

Alia, the transporter, takes you between towns. Stay within 160 px of her.

Town takes 3 s. It fails if more than 5 monsters target you.

Instances are private map copies. Most need a key and enter.

Magiport and Blink are mage skills that skip the walk.

Source: node/server.js:221-223 (B.door_dist, B.transporter_dist), 6254-6267 · Town art from the game's own guide

The world has 54 maps in G and 49 are in use. G.geometry holds the walls as line segments. The transporter lists main, winterland, desertland and cyberland. Town moves you to the main spawn point of your current map and fails with cant_escape when you are mobbed.

The main maps

Mainland: the hub, with almost all shops.

Winterland: the second town. Bees, boars, wolves, the Ice Golem.

Desertland: the third town. Scorpions, Fire Spirits, the Ent.

Grace: the hidden luck counters

Bad luck raises your next chance

Item grace

Stays on the item. Grows with higher scrolls, offerings, at random, and while the item is worn.

Player grace

One for each level. Each failure raises it. A success at that level sets it to 0.

Server grace

Shared by all players on the server. Starts at 24. Any player's success resets it.

Offering grace

Rises when an attempt with an offering fails. Falls after a success.

Ask first

Most inputs are hidden. Send upgrade with calculate: true. The server returns the exact chance and uses nothing.

Also hidden: one secret "lucky" inventory slot makes your roll better 60% of the time.

Source: node/server.js:7325-7400, 7457-7487; node/server_functions.js:44-52, 3193-3199

Grace is a pity system: it stops very long series of failures. The total that the formula uses is capped per level. The lucky slot is player.p.item_num, selected at random one time; the server log calls it "16 cheat". If the roll's first four decimals match the chance, a success gives the lucky property and achievement.

Five pieces at +5 give more stats than one piece at +7. Upgrade the weapon first at +6 and +7, because each level adds the most damage there. +8 needs a scroll1 at 15%; most players buy those items. To choose a scroll: for each, compute (value at risk + scroll price) divided by chance, and use the smaller result.

Gold and the economy

Where gold comes from, and where it goes

In

  • Monster kills: gold goes into the chest. The opener's gold multiplier applies, then 10% tax.
  • Sales to NPCs: usually 60% of the shop price.
  • Exchanges, sales to players, PvP kills.

Out (the sinks)

  • NPC purchases: potions, scrolls, gear.
  • Upgrade scrolls, and the items that failures destroy.
  • Bank packs: 75 million to 10 billion each.
  • Taxes, fees and the tavern, into the server pool.

Bots farm all day, so gold enters all the time. Without sinks, all prices go up.

Source: node/server.js:2393-2446, 8079, 1718-1725; node/server_functions.js:715-727; js/old_common_functions.js:54

Sales tax on player trades is 1% to 5%, by the level of the seller. Gold to a different account loses 2.5%; between your own characters there is no fee. Most taxes go into S.gold, the server's gold pool, which pays tavern wins. The NPC sale price is the floor for common items, and the NPC shop price is the ceiling.

A merchant in practice

Open a stand in the town square

  1. Create a merchant. Send it 40,000 gold from a fighter: no fee between your own characters.
  2. Buy a stand (stand0) from Divian at (−193, 680).
  3. Go to the square near (0, 0). Open the stand with merchant.
  4. List items above their NPC value, or the NPC is the better buyer.
  5. Keep one listing open for 2 minutes: Merrit brings a Market Parcel, once each hour.

For each item of loot, in order

Keep what you need. Compound jewelry. Send better gear to a fighter. Stand for valuable items. Sell the rest to an NPC at once.

Source: node/server.js:1756, 3468; node/logic/market_patron.js; node/server_functions.js:4282-4299

A stand has 16 trade slots, 24 for a merchant at level 70 or with a computer stand, and 30 at level 80. A character on a stand moves at speed 10, and an attack closes the stand. Close it with merchant close: true before you walk. Xyn exchanges the parcel; the most probable results are 5 upgrade scrolls, 2 compound scrolls or a high upgrade scroll. Merchants get xp from trades, donations and an open stand, not from kills.

Comic panels from the game's repository · Source: G.events.dreams; node/logic/cave_of_many_dreams.js

Bring all members to Dorr, within 160 px. Kill or leave all monsters that target a member; all members must be alive. Then send enter with place dreams. The cave gives axes, scythes and Cave Amber, which Leo crafts into rare gear. A custom client needs extra work here: each member connects with map_protocol=1, and the server sends cave events for choices and story text.

Parties

How a party shares a kill

Every member gets xp

Each member on the server gets its share of each kill, at any distance.

The share

Damage, heals ×1.8 and a quarter of damage taken, plus a fixed 36,000. Priests ×1.36. Merchants 0.

Loot

Each chest item goes to one member by share. Gold is divided by share.

Xp bonus+10%+16%+20%+24%+25%+30%+36%+40%

Also +5% gold for all, and +10 luck for each member below level 60.

Source: party_to_client node/server.js:1138-1210; issue_monster_award node/server.js:2834-2866

To make a party, send party with event invite and a name; the other side sends accept. The server adds the scores for each account and divides by the number of that account's characters in the party, so your own characters share one account's share. The contribution score decays by 20% each minute. A merchant in the party does not reduce the fighters' xp and never gets chest items.

Many characters and bots

Bots are fine. The limits are on characters.

3 + 1

Three fighters and one merchant of an account online at a time.

One account per player

All accounts of one player share the same limits.

80% for itself

An account must serve itself 80% of the time or more.

An active person

Accounts that run 24/7 with no contact with players can get a ban for a time.

No real-money trade

Do not sell gold or items for money.

Report bugs

Do not use them. A report gets a Bug Bounty Box.

From the official guide. Read the live rules before your bot depends on them.

Source: docs/guide/limits.html, htmls/contents/terms.html; is_player_allowed node/server_functions.js:383-437

The exact online limit is in a private configuration file, so the live number is unclear from the source. The server also limits players per IP address; Steam and Mac App Store accounts get a much higher limit. A Licence to Kill removes the limits for 7 minutes. Two or more online fighters end the Lone Wolf bonus.
